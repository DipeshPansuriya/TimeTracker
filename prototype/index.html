Summaries

What this prototype does not settle

Open decisions
01
Stack is still open
Deliberately. The engine above is plain logic — it ports to WPF, Electron or anything else unchanged. Pick the shell once you have clicked the flows.
02
HRMS sync has no contract
Every requirement for it is conditional on an API nobody has seen. Designing the protocol now produces guesses. The local store is built sync-ready; the transport waits.
03
Time out needs a fallback
You declare your start; the day closes when you hit Complete day. But people go home without clicking. Proposed: on next launch the widget asks what time you left the open day — never guesses it silently.
04
A tool that measures people is political
Even at pilot scale, who can see the data is a question to answer before rollout, not after. Local-first is the right default; it is not the whole answer.
